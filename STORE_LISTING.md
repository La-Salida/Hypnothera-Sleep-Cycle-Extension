## Long description

Sleep Window is a free sleep cycle calculator and bedtime calculator for Chrome. Plan when to sleep and when to wake using classic 90-minute sleep cycles — locally, privately, with no account.

**What you get**

• **Wake mode** — Enter when you need to wake up. See suggested bedtimes for 3, 4, 5, and 6 cycles.  
• **Sleep mode** — Enter when you’re going to bed, or tap Sleep now, to see best wake-up times.  
• **5-cycle highlight** — 7.5 hours is called out as a balanced default window.  
• **Fall-asleep buffer** — Adjust 0–30 minutes (default 14) so the math matches how long you usually take to drift off.  
• **Optional bedtime reminder** — Opt in to a local Chrome notification before your planned wind-down. Nothing is sent to a server.  
• **2-minute wind-down** — A short guided breathing exercise in the popup (offline visual pacer).

This is a **planning heuristic**, not medical advice, not a medical device, and not a treatment for insomnia or any condition.
