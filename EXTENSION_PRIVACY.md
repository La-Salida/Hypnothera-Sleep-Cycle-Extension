# Sleep Window — Extension privacy

**Effective for:** Sleep Window Chrome extension (Manifest V3)  
**Publisher:** Hypnothera (https://hypnothera.ai)  
**Related site policy:** https://hypnothera.ai/privacy

## Summary

Sleep Window collects **zero** user data. All calculation and preferences stay on your device.

## What stays local

- Preferred wake time  
- Minutes to fall asleep  
- Last calculator mode  
- Optional reminder on/off  
- Alarm schedule for the optional bedtime reminder  

These are stored only in `chrome.storage.local` on your browser profile.

## What we do not do

- No accounts or sign-in  
- No analytics, telemetry, or tracking pixels  
- No advertising identifiers  
- No remote scripts, CDNs, or remote fonts  
- No transmission of sleep times, preferences, or usage to Hypnothera or any third party  
- No sale or sharing of personal data (there is none collected)

## Network activity

The extension itself does not initiate network requests. Links you choose to open (for example, hypnothera.ai) are normal browser navigations you start by clicking.

## Permissions

- **storage** — local preferences only  
- **alarms** — optional on-device bedtime reminder scheduling  
- **notifications** — optional local reminder display  

## Medical disclaimer

Sleep Window is a planning heuristic for education and convenience. It is not medical advice, not a medical device, and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent insomnia or any disease.
